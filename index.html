Our farm story

Starting in Rangpur with a simple goal: grow clean food and earn trust fairly.

This project begins with one farm in Rangpur, in the Shilarpur area of Bulandshahr. The idea is to grow produce organically, pack it carefully, and sell directly to customers without depending fully on middlemen.

Over time, the platform can help more nearby farmers join and build trust through visible records, not just labels. That means healthier soil, better farmer income, and more confidence for buyers.

Proof model

Trust should come from records, timelines, and visibility.

“Organic” should not be a vague promise. This website is designed to gradually show each crop batch with details customers can actually understand.

01

Farm identity

Clear farm story, village location, and the people behind the produce.

02

Crop records

Sowing date, field section, crop stage, harvest expectation, and batch code.

03

Activity logs

Compost use, irrigation, observations, and later image updates from the field.

04

Sensor support

Arduino or ESP-based data for moisture, temperature, humidity, and soil conditions.

Questions customers may ask

A first FAQ helps make the MVP feel trustworthy and complete.

Is everything already certified organic?

This project is focused on transparent farming records and honest communication. Formal certifications can be added later, but the MVP starts by showing visible practices and real farm updates.

Can I order directly from the farm?

Yes, the first version collects direct order and interest requests so customers can connect without middlemen.

Will other farmers join too?

Later yes, but only with a structured process so trust stays strong and customers know who they are buying from.
